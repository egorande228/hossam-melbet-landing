FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? We might have already answered it. Browse the frequently asked questions below.

What is Melbet?

On this site, MelBet is presented through partnership programs for affiliates, Teamcash agents, and e-payment partners. The homepage is an overview page, and the contact form is the next step if you need current operational details.

What are the age requirements to join?

You must be 18 years or older to participate in any of MelBet's partnership programs. Additional requirements may vary depending on the country and specific program.

How does the Affiliate Program work?

The Affiliate Program is presented as a way to earn commissions by bringing new players to the MelBet platform. The overview published here lists 25% to 50% commission on player revenue through your promo code, plus an additional 3% for a master account model.

What is the Teamcash?

Teamcash is presented as a local cash deposit and withdrawal service for players in your region. The page describes it as agent work handled through the company app.

What is the E-Payment Program?

The E-Payment Program is presented for partners who handle digital payments and transfers for players. The overview says the role works with e-payment methods and includes access to technical support.

What is the Teamcash Program?

The Teamcash Program is described as a payment support role for your area. Agents help with local cash deposits and withdrawals and earn commissions on those transactions.

What is the CPA Program?

The overview says the CPA program pays a direct $10 bonus for each qualifying player you register. It is presented as a separate bonus alongside the standard affiliate commission structure.

Do you offer demo accounts for marketing?

This page says demo accounts ranging from $2,000 to $10,000 are available for marketing purposes. Use the contact page to confirm availability for your program.

What is the startup cost for becoming a Teamcash?

The Teamcash program card lists a low startup investment of $50 USD. Regional working details can be confirmed during the application process.

How do I work as a Teamcash agent?

The site presents Teamcash work through the company app. Agents process deposit and withdrawal requests for players in their area and receive commission on completed transactions.

What is the difference between a Teamcash and an E-Payment Agent?

Teamcash focuses on local cash deposit and withdrawal support, while the E-Payment Agent role focuses on digital transfers and online payment methods.

Which e-payment banks can I work with?

This overview does not publish a list of banks or providers. Use the contact form or Telegram link to confirm the supported payment methods for your country.

How and when do I receive my commissions?

The site explains that commissions depend on the partner model you choose, but it does not publish a full payout schedule on this page. Confirm payment timing and method during onboarding.

What withdrawal methods are available?

Available withdrawal and payment methods vary by program and region. The homepage presents Teamcash for local cash support and the e-payment route for digital methods.

How can I contact the partnerships team?

You can contact the partnerships team through the official contact page or the Telegram link shown on this site.

How long does the application approval process take?

The contact page says requests are reviewed and followed up on, typically within 24 hours. Timing can vary when additional review is needed.
