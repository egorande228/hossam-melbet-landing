Application overview

How the MelBet partner application process works

The MelBet partner application process on this site starts with the contact form, where applicants share their first name, last name, email address, phone number, country, preferred program, traffic source or payment experience, and a short message about goals and target GEOs. The form is designed for the same three routes published on the homepage: affiliate, Teamcash, and e-payment partnership opportunities. According to the published copy, the MelBet Partnerships team reviews each request and typically follows up within 24 hours. This page does not list country-specific payout methods, provider availability, or final onboarding terms, so those details are confirmed during application review. If you still need to compare the programs, use the homepage summaries and FAQ first, then submit the form with the route and region that best match your experience.

The homepage remains the canonical public overview for the three program types, while this page is the published application path for follow-up and current requirements.

Before you submit

Choose the partner route that matches your experience

These summaries only restate the three published options on the homepage so you can select the most relevant program before opening the form.

Affiliate

Best fit for audience growth and traffic acquisition

  • Use this route if you promote MelBet and want to earn commission on referred player activity.
  • The homepage lists a 25% to 50% commission range, a possible 3% master-account uplift, and a $10 CPA bonus.
  • Share your traffic source, audience channels, and target GEOs in the application message.
Review affiliate details

Teamcash

Best fit for local cash deposits and withdrawals

  • Use this route if you want to support player deposits and withdrawals in your region.
  • The published program summary says agents work through the company app, must be 18+, and start from a $50 USD investment.
  • Use your message to explain the country you plan to support and any payment-operations background you already have.
Review Teamcash details

E-payment

Best fit for digital transfers and payment operations

  • Use this route if your experience is closer to digital transfers and online payment support.
  • The homepage presents this option as a commission-based payment role with technical support and reporting access.
  • Use the form to request current country-level confirmation for supported methods and onboarding requirements.
